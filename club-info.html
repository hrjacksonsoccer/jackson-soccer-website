Mission & Vision

Jackson Soccer Club aspires to be an outstanding educational-athletic organization that provides a high-quality experience to every athlete and promotes the positives of team sports. A high-quality experience is one in which every athlete:

  • Learns the skills, tactics, and strategies of the game and improves as a player
  • Is challenged to improve and achieve as a player
  • Shares positive and valuable experiences with friends
  • Feels like an important part of the team regardless of performance
  • Learns "life lessons" that have value beyond the playing field
  • Is coached using the principles of Positive Coaching
  • Has fun at practices and games

Mastering the skills of the game and having fun go hand-in-hand with developing a lifelong interest in soccer and all team sports. Our goal is to encourage every child to have fun and return to the program year after year. We also hope that all parents will enjoy their experience and feel rewarded by their involvement. We want players and families to feel they are part of a program and not just on a team.

We recognize that coaches are the people who most directly make this all possible. It is our goal to provide every coach with the tools to succeed and to grow as coaches and role models. We are committed to creating a positive culture, in which coaches, trainers, parents, fans, officials and athletes work together to achieve our mission.

Financial Assistance

Jackson Soccer Club never wants cost to be the reason a child cannot play. Families who need help with registration fees may apply for financial assistance.

Applications are reviewed confidentially by the board. Assistance is awarded based on demonstrated need and the funds available for that season, and it applies to registration fees only — uniforms, equipment, and travel-team costs are not covered.

Families who receive assistance are asked to give back to the club through volunteer hours during the season (for example helping at the fields, at events, or with equipment). Please apply before the registration deadline for the season you are applying for; late applications may not be able to be processed in time for team placement.

Peer Mentor Program

The Peer Mentor program pairs older players with our youngest members, giving Peanuts and Future Stars an extra set of hands on the field and giving teen players a chance to lead.

Peer Mentors help the trainers and coaches run Saturday morning sessions — setting up drills, demonstrating skills, and encouraging the little ones. Sessions run Saturday mornings during the season, alongside the regular 8:30 AM to noon program.

Peer Mentors must be born in an eligible birth year for the program (roughly middle-school and high-school age) and must have parental permission. Volunteer hours served as a Peer Mentor can be used toward school community service requirements — let us know and we will sign off on your hours.
