Financial Assistance

Help with registration fees for Jackson families

Jackson Soccer Club aspires to be an outstanding educational-athletic organization that provides a high-quality experience to every athlete, regardless of their financial circumstances, and promotes the positives of team sports.

The Jackson Soccer Club offers assistance to a limited number of families who have a financial hardship in regard to the payment of registration. Assistance can take the form of funds awarded or modified payment plans to spread out the burden. All assistance is based on financial need or hardship. The awarding of funds is determined solely by a financial assistance committee appointed by the Board of Directors.

To be eligible for financial assistance, players must be registered in the system for either Travel or Intramural.

🔒 Your information stays confidential. All information obtained from this application is held in strict confidence and will only be reviewed by the Financial Assistance Committee. The primary criteria for evaluation will be the family's eligibility for or participation in the Free/Reduced lunch program.

Request Procedures

  1. All hardship requests must be submitted through our online form. No requests will be taken by email or word of mouth.
  2. The application for financial assistance is always open but is determined by the amount of money available. This includes new players joining teams mid-year. It is important to apply early.
  3. All families who receive financial assistance are required to give back to the program by volunteering a minimum of 10 hours of time during each season that they receive aid.
  4. It is the belief of the board that everyone can afford something. Full financial aid is rare. All players are expected to register with the club online registration site following tryouts to secure their spot on the team. Players not registered in the online system will not be eligible for any financial assistance.
  5. Funds are limited and based on the number of registrations and donations.
  6. Assistance is not guaranteed every year. Families must apply each year for any players that are in need.
  7. Financial aid money is credited off the back end of registration. Financial aid will be awarded in full or partial amounts, or may be rejected. Payment plans may be approved by the Committee on a case by case basis.
  8. Financial aid will take the place of the sibling discounts should there be additional family members in the club.
  9. Financial aid awarded is applied to tuition only. No aid will be awarded to cover the cost of uniforms, camps, travel costs or variable fees for additional events in which a team participates.
